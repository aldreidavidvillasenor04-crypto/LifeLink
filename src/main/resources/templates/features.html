THE PLATFORM

Tools for the donation journey

One connected workspace helps donors and authorized staff keep the process organized.

01

Donor management

Maintain donor profiles, contact details, and blood type information in one place.

02

Health assessment

Record screening responses and keep assessment information connected to the donor for staff review.

03

Eligibility review

Present preliminary assessment outcomes for qualified personnel to review before donation.

04

Donation history

Track appointments and donation records so staff can review a donor's history.

Screening note

LifeLink supports record keeping. Automated assessment results do not replace professional screening or final medical clearance.
