OUR PURPOSE

About LifeLink

LifeLink helps communities coordinate blood donation with clear donor records, health screening, and reliable donation history.

WHY WE EXIST

Make every donation easier to organize.

Finding the right donor and keeping accurate records takes coordination. LifeLink brings donor information, assessments, appointments, and donation records together so authorized teams can work from a consistent view.

♥

People first

Screening decisions belong to qualified staff. LifeLink supports their work with organized information and a clear record of each step.

What guides the service

Care

Respect donor privacy and make important next steps clear.

Accuracy

Keep donor and donation records associated with the correct person.

Coordination

Help staff and donors follow the donation process in an orderly way.
